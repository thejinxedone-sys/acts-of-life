Acts of Life · Style specification · v1

One world, five zoom levels.

Every screen is the same world at a different focal length. The design carries this in four ways: a shared field of telescope rings that rescales with each level, three glyphs that stand for the three modes without words, type that loosens as you zoom out, and a blur-through camera move on every transition. Tokens live in acts-of-life-tokens.css; built on the Organic design system.

1 · The glyph language

Labour, Work and Action are never spelled out in the interface — the mark says it. One mark, three lives; the mode transition morphs between them.

The loop LABOUR · hue 145
Anchors you tend so everything else can move. Rituals close the ring daily; a done ritual snaps shut (loopPop). Lists bow along a dashed arc; the day's ring fills as loops close.
The line WORK · hue 240
Paths you extend, that outlast the day — streams with phases and milestone notches. Progress literally lengthens the stroke on the horizon. Social roles live here: acts that grow a life.
The star ACTION · hue 320
Acts that define your life. The five principles are stars — acting on one makes it flare (starIgnite); reflection lights the week's constellation on the night ground.

Triad rule: same OKLCH lightness (58%) and chroma (0.08) — only hue varies. Each mode carries four steps: base · ink · tint · bright (bright is the dark-ground variant). Never use a mode color as a full-bleed chip fill; it appears as strokes, dots, thin bars and tints.

2 · Grounds — day and deep space

Close zoom levels (Today, Stream) live on warm cream; the widest levels (Life horizon, Evening reflection) live on warm near-black with faint rings and star specks. Zooming out is literally moving into night sky.

--ground #f5ead8
--ground-raised
--surface-sunken
--space-ground
--ember #c67139
--ember-bright

Ember is the only warm accent and it means now / begin: the today line, the + button, primary actions, principle stars at night (as --ember-bright). The telescope rings are 1.5px circles at 3–6% ink, radiating from the top third; they grow one notch per zoom-out.

3 · Type — three voices

Today's ActsCaprasimo 400 — the big-picture voice. Screen titles, principles, onboarding statements. Never below 16px, never for UI labels.
Meditate for 15 minFigtree — the working voice. Items 15/600, body 13/400, buttons and chips 13–14/600.
Labour · The Loop You KeepMonospace — the instrument voice. Kickers (10px, .14em tracking, uppercase), meta lines (11px), dates, readouts ("1/4 loops closed"). This voice is what makes the app feel like a lens.

4 · Motion — the zoom grammar

zoom in
scale .93 → 1 · opacity .25 → 1 · blur 5px → 0 · .45s ease. Every step down the ladder.
zoom out
scale 1.08 → 1 · blur 6px → 0 · .5s. Arriving at the horizon.
loopPop
checkbox snaps shut: scale .5 rot −120° → 1.15 → 1, .5s. Ritual completion only.
starIgnite
flare to 1.25 + 22° at the 88% mark of a 5s loop. Principle stars, gently alive.
spinSlow
360° in 40s, linear — the Health loop schematic turning on the horizon.
sheetUp
translateY 40px → 0, .3s. Bottom sheets (add act/stream, set dates).

Rule: motion only ever means one of two things — a change of focal length, or a mark coming alive. Nothing else animates.

5 · Component rules

Cards — 24px radius, --surface-card fill, 1px --edge, shadow-sm. One card style everywhere; sections differ only by mono kickers.
Pills — every button, chip, input and the evening bar is fully round (999px).
Checkboxes — 26px circles stroked in the mode color; done = filled + cream check + loopPop.
Glyph nav — 44px round buttons, active = mode tint fill; the masthead kicker and tagline change with it.
Horizon — bars 6px in mode colors, milestone dots 8px (filled = done), ember today-line, past region shaded, act-dots trail behind / hollow ahead.
Copy voice — grounded, declarative, philosophical but actionable: "A life of action, tended daily" · "the anchors you tend so everything else can move" · "Did you act today?" Acknowledgement to Hannah Arendt lives in onboarding.
